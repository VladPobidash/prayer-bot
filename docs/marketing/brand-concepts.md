# Prayer Circle — launch copy and visual directions

This document is a selection board for the bot's public-facing identity. Nothing
here changes the bot automatically; the chosen name, description, and avatar
must be applied in BotFather after approval.

## Recommended bot name

**Prayer Circle**

Short promise: **Pray daily. Grow together.**

The name is warm, communal, and immediately explains the product. It works for
a church, ministry, family, or small group without sounding like an
administration tool.

Other strong directions, if a more distinctive name is preferred:

- **Together in Prayer** — warm and explicit, but longer.
- **Daily Prayer Circle** — clear and habit-focused, but less ownable.
- **OnePrayer** — compact and modern, but more abstract.

> Naming note: check Telegram username and relevant trademark availability
> before public launch. The display name does not have to match the username.

## “What can this bot do?” description

### Recommended final copy

**Turn good intentions into a shared prayer rhythm. Create a private prayer
circle, invite your people, and care for shared and personal requests—all inside
Telegram. Get a gentle daily reminder, see today’s prayer focus, celebrate
answered prayers, and encourage one another with voice or video. One tap marks
your prayer complete. No noisy group chat—just a simple, meaningful habit you
build together.**

### Compact alternative

**Build a daily prayer habit—together. Create a private circle, share prayer
requests, receive a personal daily focus, celebrate answered prayers, and send
voice or video encouragement. Gentle reminders and one-tap check-ins keep your
group connected without adding noise to the group chat.**

## Voice principles

- **Inviting, not demanding:** use “gentle reminder,” “encourage,” and
  “together,” rather than guilt or pressure.
- **Human, not technical:** lead with the shared ritual, not rooms, assignments,
  databases, or settings.
- **Specific, not inflated:** promise only the private circles, daily focus,
  reminders, answered-prayer tracking, and media encouragement available now.
- **Active and concise:** “Create. Invite. Pray.” is the core narrative.

## Avatar concepts

All concepts are square, text-free, and designed with a centered safe area for
Telegram's circular avatar crop. SVG masters are resolution-independent and
can be exported to a 1,024 × 1,024 PNG after selection.

### Option A — The Living Circle (recommended)

![The Living Circle](../../assets/brand/logo-option-a-living-circle.svg)

A radiant prayer flame held inside a continuous circle. The circle communicates
belonging and consistency; the upward flame feels hopeful, alive, and
spiritual. Deep indigo and vivid coral make it recognizable at small sizes.

**Best for:** the most welcoming, emotionally resonant identity.

### Option B — The Prayer Signal

![The Prayer Signal](../../assets/brand/logo-option-b-prayer-signal.svg)

A modern Telegram-like conversation shape becomes a glowing sanctuary window,
with three rising rays suggesting prayer, response, and connection. Electric
blue and warm gold feel confident and digital without becoming corporate.

**Best for:** a bolder, more product-led identity.

### Option C — Together, Upward

![Together, Upward](../../assets/brand/logo-option-c-together-upward.svg)

Three people form a single upward-moving symbol around a small central spark.
The mark emphasizes community and shared momentum while staying abstract enough
to work across cultures and denominations.

**Best for:** a community-first identity with a premium, contemporary feel.

## Recommended launch combination

- **Name:** Prayer Circle
- **Tagline:** Pray daily. Grow together.
- **Description:** Recommended final copy above
- **Avatar:** Option A — The Living Circle
- **Primary colors:** Midnight `#17143B`, violet `#6C4BFF`, coral `#FF5E70`,
  and warm cream `#FFF4DC`
